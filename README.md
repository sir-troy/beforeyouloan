# Before You Loan

Know the true cost of a loan before you sign.

**Live:** https://beforeyouloan.useful-indeed.com

Before You Loan shows the real cost of a loan before you commit to it. Enter your income, expenses and the loan's terms, and it tells you the total amount you'll pay back, the effective interest rate, how the monthly payment fits your budget, what happens if you pay late, and safer alternatives to consider. It speaks Taglish, like most of us do.

## How to use

1. Type your monthly take-home pay, other income and current expenses.
2. Enter the loan amount, interest per month, number of months and any processing fee.
3. Choose the interest type (add-on/flat or diminishing) and the purpose of the loan.
4. Read the result: total payable, true interest rate, budget impact and late-payment scenarios.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is stored. Close the tab and it's gone.
